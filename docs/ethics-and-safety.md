# Ethics, safety, and responsible research

- Use only models and datasets under terms that permit the research.
- Follow institutional ethics and data-governance requirements.
- Do not include personal or confidential information in prompts, logs, or commits.
- Store sensitive evaluation content in access-controlled locations.
- Keep threat-model descriptions and benchmark references at the level needed for reproducibility without publishing operational instructions that facilitate harm.
- Evaluate over-refusal and benign utility alongside refusal behavior.
- Report limitations, uncertainty, failed runs, and negative results.
- Do not claim that an intervention makes a model safe in general based on a narrow benchmark.
